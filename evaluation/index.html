📝 Who gave how much

Each column is one investor team and must add up to 100 points. Copy the amount from the bottom of each sheet. The grey cells stay empty: a team never invests in itself. Everything is saved in this browser.

🏆 Ranking

On equal totals, the team that received points from more investors ranks higher. If it is still equal, the trainers decide.
