What is the task?

Imagine that your team is a small company that advises investors. You received a big Excel table with the data of 1000 young companies. Your company has one single question to answer:

Which startup deserves our trust the most?
🌱 What is a startup?A newly founded business that wants to grow fast. It often spends a lot of money before it makes a profit, so it is hard to tell which one will succeed.
🎭 Every company is made up!The numbers are artificial. Nothing real is at stake, but the way of thinking is the same as in real life.

🗺️ The road from the Excel table to the decision

🧩 What is already done, and what do you build?

🏁 Your job is the web page!The blue parts (reading the data, scoring, the connection to the AI) are already done. Your company builds the red web page through which everything can be used. Then you decide which startup is the best, and you present it. Only one person in your team writes the page (with the AI's help): the developer. Everyone else has a different job. See page 8.

🔑 The six most important criteria, in simple words

📈 Growth

How much did the revenue of the company grow compared to last year?

Like a snack bar whose sales grew by half in one year.

💰 Profitability (EBITDA margin)

If the company takes in 100 forints, how much is left after the running costs? If it is negative, the company loses money.

Like your monthly pocket money: income minus spending.

🧾 Gross margin

From a product sold for 100 forints, how much is left after the cost of making it?

Lemonade: sold for 100 Ft, ingredients cost 30 Ft, so the margin is 70%.

⏳ Runway

For how many months can the company live on its current cash, if no more money came in?

Like the battery of your phone: how many hours are left?

🤝 Customer loyalty

What share of the customers stays? The Excel shows the churn (customers who leave): the lower, the better.

Like the regulars of a cafe: how many come back?

🏦 Funding

How much money have investors trusted the company with so far?

If many people gave money, they probably believe in it.

🪜 The maturity of a company: funding stages

Like school grades: the further right, the more mature the company. This is how the 1000 startups are spread out:

The companies work in 10 fields, for example EdTech (education), HealthTech, FinTech (finance), AgriTech (farming), CleanTech and ClimateTech (environment), Cybersecurity, Logistics, E-commerce (web shops) and AI / SaaS (software).

How the program scores

The program gives every startup a score between 0 and 100. It works like a school report: several subjects make up the average, and some subjects count more than others.

1
Points. Each of the six criteria gets 0 to 100 points. The better the company is at it, the more points.
2
Weight. Not every criterion is equally important. Growth is worth 25%, customer loyalty only 10%.
3
Adding up. We add up the weighted points. This is the total score of the company.

Risk and weights

Next to the score, the program shows a traffic light for every company. It tells how likely the company is to get into trouble. Like a real traffic light: red, yellow or green.

💡 Why does this matter?A company can have a high score and still a red light. For a good decision, look at both.

The web page you build

The web page is what people see and use: buttons, tables, sliders. It has two levels: what is a must, and what is nice if there is time.

🔴 Minimum: this must work
  • 📥 Excel upload: a button that loads the 1000 startups.
  • 🏆 Ranking: a table with each company's name, score and risk light.
  • 🤖 Ask the AI: a text box where you type a question and the answer appears.

  • ⇄ Comparison: two startups side by side.
  • 📊 Charts: revenue, sectors or the top 5.
  • 🚦 Colored risk light: red, yellow, green in the table.
  • 🔎 Search and filter: by company or by sector.
  • ✨ Good looks: colors, cards, your own style.

6

The role of the AI

The AI is a language model (Google Gemini). It is like a very fast, well-read colleague: it reads the data of the 1000 startups and answers your questions. The question box of your web page reaches it.

🔄 What happens when you ask a question?

🧮 The program calculatesThe ranking and the scores are calculated by the program, always the same way. This is not done by the AI.
🗣️ The AI explainsThe AI interprets the result, gives reasons, compares and answers your questions.

💬 Questions to start with

💬 Which are the 5 most promising startups?
💬 Which companies have the highest financial risk?
💬 Compare the best three companies!
💬 Why did the best company get the first place?
💬 In which sector are the most stable companies?
💬 Whom would you recommend to a cautious investor?

A good question is specific. Do not only ask "What is the best?", also ask "Why?" and "What could be wrong with it?". By default the AI answers in Hungarian, ask your trainer if you need English.

👍 It is good at

Summarizing quickly, explaining, comparing, giving ideas and writing in a simple way.

⚠️ Be careful!

The AI can be wrong and say a wrong thing with confidence. Always check every important number in the table!

🔑 The AI needs a secret keyThe trainer tells you how to set up a key. It is like a password: do not share it, and never put it on a slide!

Your small company: roles

You are a small company: everybody has a job and depends on the others. Your team has 5 to 7 people, and only one of you is the developer.

🔁 How the work flows in a small company

🎭 The roles

🧭 Manager
Sets goals and deadlines, keeps time, removes blockers and makes the final call.
🤖 With AI: asks for a plan and a checklist
💻 Developer
The ONLY person who builds the page. Works from the spec and design. No coding skills needed: you describe, the AI writes.
🤖 With AI: builds the page, pastes errors to fix them
📝 Spec writer
Turns ideas into clear written requirements: minimum first, then nice-to-haves. Answers the developer.
🤖 With AI: drafts and sharpens the requirements
🎨 Designer
Sketches the layout, picks colors and style, reviews the result, later designs the slides.
🤖 With AI: asks for layout and color ideas
💰 Financial analyst
Understands the criteria, picks the investor profile and weights, finds the winner, judges risks.
🤖 With AI: asks the startup questions in the app
🧪 Tester
Writes a checklist from the spec, tests the page, reports bugs, checks the AI's claims.
🤖 With AI: turns the spec into a checklist
🎤 Presenter
Builds the story, speaks for the team and turns everyone's results into a pitch.
🤖 With AI: suggests slide texts and titles
✨ Your own role
Invent another role your company needs, for example customer support or legal.
👥 How many people are you?5 people: the Manager also presents and the Spec writer also tests. 6 people: add the Tester. 7 people: add the Presenter, so everyone has one role.

10

The presentation

In the second 80 minutes you present your solution to the other teams. It does not have to be perfect, but it needs a story: where you started, what you found and where you ended up.

🎞️ The suggested story: 5 slides

Slides 1-2: the base

What did you look at? Which criteria did you find important, and why?

Slide 3: the winner

Who won? Show it with a chart or a picture of your ranking and tell why this one.

Slide 4: the risks

What could go wrong with the winner? Did the winner stay the same when you changed a weight?

Slide 5: the AI

How did the AI help, and where did you have to correct it? What would you do differently next time?

⭐ What to pay attention to

👤 Understandable

Speak so that anyone who was not with you can follow.

🧠 Reasoned

Explain why you chose these weights.

🎭 Honest

Show the risks, and where the AI can be wrong.

🎨 Visual

Use pictures, colors and numbers, not long texts.

🏢 Show your company!Let every role say one sentence about what they contributed. It shows that you worked as a real team.
✅ Before you present, ask yourselvesWould someone who hears it for the first time understand it? Is every number true? Does everybody have a part in the show?

Good to know and glossary

🚫 Not investment adviceThe score and the ranking are only estimates to help you think. They are not enough for a real financial decision.
🎭 The data is made upThe companies do not exist, the numbers were generated. Any similarity to real companies is a coincidence.
🤖 The AI can be wrongAlways check the important numbers before you put a claim on a slide.
🔑 The key is a secretDo not copy the key of the AI anywhere, and do not show it on the screen.

📚 Small glossary

StartupA new business that wants to grow fast.
SectorThe field the company works in (for example health or finance).
EBITDAThe profit from running the business. If negative, the company loses money.
Gross marginThe part of the selling price that is left after the costs.
RunwayFor how many months the company's money is enough.
ChurnThe share of customers who leave the company.
WeightHow important a criterion is.
RankingThe companies in order of their score.
M FtMillion forints. Money amounts are given in this.
PromptThe question or instruction you write to the AI.
FE (front end)The web page that the user sees and uses.
APIA gate through which the web page asks the back end for data.
SpecA written description of what the page must do.
